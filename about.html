About Us

Learn about our journey, mission, and expertise

Our Story

Established in 2013, Indian Material Research Center (IMRC) has emerged as a leading organization specializing in Geotechnical, Geophysical, Geological Investigations, Traffic Surveys, Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), Pavement Design, and Concrete Mix Design for land, marine, and water-based projects.

Headquartered in Kharghar, Navi Mumbai, we are committed to delivering high-quality material testing services at competitive prices. Over the years, IMRC has built up a competent global Services with consistent and high quality service, allowing us to complete your task with the most efficient high-speed service.

With a robust team of experienced professionals and state-of-the-art technology, IMRC has successfully catered to numerous public and private sector projects, becoming a trusted name in material research and testing. We uphold the highest industry standards, ensuring our results align with Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), ASTM, IRC, and other international regulations.
