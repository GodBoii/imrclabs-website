Geotechnical Investigation Services

IMRC Labs offers comprehensive geotechnical investigation services for land, marine, and water-based projects. Our experienced team provides detailed subsurface exploration and analysis to support safe and efficient foundation design for various construction and infrastructure projects.

Our Geotechnical Investigation Services

  • Geotechnical Investigation reports with recommendations
  • Safe bearing capacity calculations
  • Standard Penetration Test
  • Dynamic Cone Penetration Test
  • Field Vane Shear Test
  • Static Plate Load Test
  • Cyclic Plate Load Test
  • Modulus of Subgrade Reaction
  • Pressure Meter Test
  • Permeability in overburden and in Rock by single packer and Double packer
  • Inclinometer Installation
  • Piezometer Installation
  • Cross Hole Seismic
  • Electrical Resistivity Test
  • Consultancy for Soil Stabilization
  • Slope Stability Calculation

Applications of Geotechnical Investigation

  • Building foundations
  • Highway and railway infrastructure
  • Bridges and tunnels
  • Dams and embankments
  • Marine structures
  • Industrial facilities
  • Slope stability assessment

Our geotechnical investigation services have supported numerous high-profile projects, including the High-Speed Rail Project between Mumbai and Ahmedabad. We provide accurate data and actionable recommendations to ensure the safety and stability of your structures.
