Soil Testing Services

IMRC Labs provides comprehensive soil testing services to support geotechnical investigations and construction projects. Our soil tests include:

  • Liquid /Plastic Limit/Plasticity Index
  • Specific Gravity
  • Shrinkage Limit
  • Standard Proctor Test
  • Modified Proctor Test
  • Relative Density
  • Lab C.B.R. Test

  • Consolidation test
