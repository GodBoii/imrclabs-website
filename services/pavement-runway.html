Pavement & Runway Testing Services

IMRC Labs provides specialized testing services for pavements, roads, and runways to ensure their structural integrity, durability, and performance. Our comprehensive testing solutions help in evaluating existing conditions, monitoring performance, and designing new pavements or rehabilitation strategies.

Our Pavement & Runway Testing Services

  • Benkelman Beam Test for Bituminous Road
  • Axle Load Survey
  • K-value Tests (Modulus of Subgrade Reaction)
  • Deformation Modulus
  • Traffic Survey
  • Field CBR Tests
  • Dynamic Cone Penetration Test (DCPT)
  • Pavement Design for Flexible and Rigid Pavement
  • Deformation/Strain Modulus (Ev1 and Ev2) as per DIN 18134

Material Testing for Pavements

  • Mix design for sub base, Granular sub base
  • Wet Mix Macadam, WBM, Mastic Asphalt
  • Mix Design Asphalt, Asphalt Concrete
  • Dry Lean Concrete
  • Bitumen, Modified Bitumen and Bitumen Mix

Applications

Our pavement and runway testing services are applicable to:

  • Highways and urban roads
  • Airport runways and taxiways
  • Industrial pavements
  • Port pavements
  • Rehabilitation and maintenance projects
  • New construction projects
