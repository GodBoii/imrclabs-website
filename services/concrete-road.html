Concrete & Road Testing Services

IMRC Labs offers comprehensive testing services for concrete and road materials to ensure structural integrity, durability, and compliance with industry standards. Our state-of-the-art facilities and experienced professionals deliver accurate and reliable results for construction and infrastructure projects.

Fresh & Hardened Concrete Testing

  • Concrete Mix Design
  • Air Entrainment Test
  • Bleeding, Air content, workability tests
  • Admixture Dosage Fixation
  • Compression tests on cubes, cylinder and prizm
  • Splitting Tensile Strength on Cubes and cylinders
  • Flexural Test
  • Modulus of Elasticity
  • Core Capping and testing
  • Concrete permeability test
  • Chemical Analysis
  • Chloride Profile

Road & Runway Testing

  • Mix design for sub base, Granular sub base, Wet Mix Macadam, WBM, Mastic Asphalt
  • Mix Design Asphalt, Asphalt Concrete, Dry Lean Concrete
  • Benkelman Beam Test for Bituminous Road
  • Dynamic Cone Penetration Test (DCPT)
  • Field CBR
  • Modulus of Subgrade Reaction (K-Value)
  • Deformation/Strain Modulus (Ev1 and Ev2) as per DIN 18134
  • Axle Load Survey
  • Traffic Survey
  • Pavement Design for Flexible and Rigid Pavement
  • Plate load test
  • Standard Penetration Test
