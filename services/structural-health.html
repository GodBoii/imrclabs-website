Structural Health Monitoring Services

IMRC Labs provides comprehensive structural health monitoring services to assess and monitor the condition of various structures over time. Our advanced monitoring techniques help identify potential issues early, allowing for timely intervention and ensuring the safety and longevity of your structures.

Our Structural Health Monitoring Services

  • Crack Width Measurement
  • Vibration Analysis
  • Deflection Measurement
  • Corrosion Analysis
  • Half-cell Potentiometer Testing
  • Concrete Resistivity Test
  • Carbonation Test
  • Cover Meter Test
  • Concrete Core Extraction and Testing
  • Load Testing of Structural Elements

Benefits of Structural Health Monitoring

  • Early detection of structural problems
  • Prevention of catastrophic failures
  • Extension of structure service life
  • Reduced maintenance costs through timely intervention
  • Enhanced safety for building occupants
  • Data-driven maintenance planning

Our structural health monitoring services can be applied to a wide range of structures, including buildings, bridges, dams, tunnels, and other infrastructure. We provide both one-time assessments and ongoing monitoring programs tailored to your specific needs.
