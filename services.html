Our Services

Comprehensive material testing, investigation, and assessment solutions

Material Testing & Quality Assurance

Comprehensive testing services ensure compliance with industry standards and quality assurance requirements for various construction and industrial materials.

Soil Testing

  • Grain size analysis with Hydrometer
  • Liquid Limit / Plastic Limit / Plasticity Index
  • Specific Gravity
  • Shrinkage Limit
  • Standard Proctor Test (Moisture-Density Relationship)
  • Modified Proctor Test (Moisture-Density Relationship)
  • Relative Density
  • Laboratory California Bearing Ratio (CBR) Test
  • Triaxial Compression Test (UU, CU, CD)
  • Consolidation Test
  • Permeability Test (Constant Head, Falling Head)
  • Direct Shear Test
  • Chemical Analysis (pH, Sulphate, Chloride, Organic Matter etc.)
  • Free Swell Index
  • Swelling Pressure Test

Rock Testing

  • Unconfined Compressive Strength (UCS)
  • Density (Bulk & Dry)
  • Porosity
  • Water Absorption
  • Young's Modulus (E-value) and Poisson's ratio
  • Point Load Strength Index
  • Triaxial Compressive Test
  • Soundness Test
  • Slake Durability Test
  • Brazilian Test (Tensile Strength)
  • Cherchar Abrasivity Index
  • Moh's Hardness (Scratch Hardness)

Building Material Testing

  • Cement, Fly ash, Microsilica & Ground Granulated Blast-furnace Slag (GGBS) Testing
  • Coarse Aggregates and Fine Aggregates (Sand)
  • Bricks (Clay & Fly Ash)
  • Solid Concrete Blocks
  • Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) Blocks
  • Paver Blocks
  • Concrete / Mosaic / Checkered Tiles
  • Ceramic Tiles
  • Timber & Plywood
  • Doors
  • Rubble / Stone
  • Marble & Granite
  • Water for Construction Purposes
  • Concrete Admixtures
  • Kerb Stones & Water Tables
  • Bitumen, Modified Bitumen, and Bituminous Mixes

Chemical Analysis

Water Analysis:

  • Construction Water
  • Drinking Water
  • STP/ETP Effluent Water
  • Ground Water
  • Specialized Waters (Swimming Pool, Agriculture, Industrial, Flushing, Irrigation, Gardening, Laundry)

Building Material Analysis:

  • Steel (Reinforcement Bars)
  • Aggregate
  • Cement
  • Fly-Ash
  • GGBS
  • Micro silica / Alcofine
  • Gypsum
  • Lime
  • Soil (Chemical properties)
  • Concrete (Hardened)
  • Admixture
  • Refractory Material

Mineral & Ore Analysis:

  • Calcite, Dolomite, Feldspar, Mica, Soapstone, Zeolite
  • Magnesite, Talc, Limestone, Serpentine, Bentonite, Wollastonite
  • Quartz, Sand and Foundry Sand
  • Ores: Iron ore, Manganese ore, Bauxite
  • Coal / Coke

Inorganic Chemical Analysis:

  • Acids
  • Soda Ash
  • Sodium Hydroxide (Caustic Soda, Flake/Lye)
  • Bleaching Powder
  • Sodium Hexametaphosphate, Sodium Silicate
  • Dry Powder, Rock Phosphate, Ferric Alums
  • Edible Salts, Table Salts, Flux, etc.

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

  • Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) Test
  • Rebound Hammer Test
  • Half-cell Potentiometer Test (Corrosion Mapping)
  • Carbonation Depth Test
  • Pile Integrity Testing (PIT)
  • Cover Meter Test (Rebar Location & Cover Depth)
  • Concrete Resistivity Test
  • Core Extraction and Testing (Semi-destructive)

Concrete & Road Material Testing

Fresh & Hardened Concrete Testing:

  • Concrete Mix Design (Various Grades)
  • Air Entrainment Test
  • Bleeding, Air Content, Workability Tests (Slump, Compaction Factor, Vee-Bee)
  • Admixture Dosage Fixation
  • Compression Tests (Cubes, Cylinders, Prisms)
  • Splitting Tensile Strength (Cubes, Cylinders)
  • Flexural Strength Test (Beams)
  • Modulus of Elasticity
  • Core Capping and Testing
  • Concrete Permeability Test (RCPT, Water Permeability)
  • Chemical Analysis of Hardened Concrete
  • Chloride Profile Analysis

Road & Runway Material Testing:

  • Mix Design (Sub base, Granular Sub Base - GSB, Wet Mix Macadam - WMM, Water Bound Macadam - WBM, Mastic Asphalt)
  • Mix Design (Asphalt Concrete - AC / Bituminous Concrete - BC, Dense Bituminous Macadam - DBM, Dry Lean Concrete - DLC)
  • Bitumen Testing (Penetration, Softening Point, Ductility etc.)
  • Modified Bitumen Testing
  • Bitumen Mix Testing (Marshall Stability, Flow, Extraction etc.)

Engineering & Structural Assessments

Comprehensive engineering and structural assessment services to evaluate existing infrastructure, monitor performance, ensure safety, and guide design or rehabilitation strategies.

Pavement & Runway Testing (Field)

  • Benkelman Beam Deflection Test (Bituminous Roads)
  • Axle Load Survey
  • Modulus of Subgrade Reaction (K-value) Tests (Plate Bearing Test)
  • Deformation Modulus / Strain Modulus (Ev1 and Ev2) as per DIN 18134 (Plate Bearing Test)
  • Traffic Volume Count Survey
  • Field California Bearing Ratio (CBR) Tests
  • Dynamic Cone Penetration Test (DCPT)
  • Pavement Design (Flexible and Rigid Pavement) based on survey data

Geotechnical Investigation (Field & Analysis)

  • Conducting Boreholes (Land, Marine, Water-based)
  • Standard Penetration Test (SPT)
  • Dynamic Cone Penetration Test (DCPT)
  • Field Vane Shear Test
  • Static Plate Load Test
  • Cyclic Plate Load Test
  • Pressure Meter Test
  • Field Permeability Test (Overburden & Rock via Single/Double Packer)
  • Inclinometer Installation & Monitoring
  • Piezometer Installation & Monitoring
  • Cross Hole Seismic Test
  • Electrical Resistivity Test
  • Geotechnical Investigation Reports with Recommendations
  • Safe Bearing Capacity (SBC) Calculations
  • Pile Capacity Calculations
  • Settlement Analysis
  • Slope Stability Calculation & Analysis
  • Consultancy for Soil Stabilization

Structural Health Monitoring (SHM)

  • Crack Width Measurement & Mapping
  • Vibration Analysis & Monitoring
  • Deflection Measurement & Monitoring (Structural Elements)
  • Corrosion Analysis (using NDT methods like Half-cell)
  • Strain Gauge Installation & Monitoring
  • Load Testing of Structural Elements (Beams, Slabs etc.)
  • Long-term monitoring using sensors
  • Pile Load Testing (Static, Lateral, Dynamic)
  • Bridge Load Testing
  • Slab Load Testing

Condition Assessment

  • Visual Inspection & Assessment (RCC, PCC, Steel Structures)
  • Evaluation of Buildings, Bridges, and Under-Water Structures
  • Quality Checks / Audits on Field during construction
  • Moisture Meter Test
  • Assessment of Raising Dampness
  • Thickness Testing of Steel Structures (Ultrasonic Thickness Gauge)
  • Detailed Condition Assessment Reports with repair/retrofitting recommendations
