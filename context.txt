About Us
Established in 2013, Indian Material Research Center (IMRC) has emerged as a leading organization specializing in Geotechnical, Geophysical, Geological Investigations, Traffic Surveys, Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), Pavement Design, and Concrete Mix Design for land, marine, and water-based projects. Headquartered in Kharghar, Navi Mumbai, we are committed to delivering high-quality material testing services at competitive prices.
With a robust team of experienced professionals and state-of-the-art technology, IMRC has successfully catered to numerous public and private sector projects, becoming a trusted name in material research and testing. We uphold the highest industry standards, ensuring our results align with Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), ASTM, IRC, and other international regulations.

Our Services
Material Testing & Quality Assurance
Soil Testing: Grain size analysis, proctor tests, permeability tests, triaxial compression tests, CBR tests, and more.
Rock Testing: Compressive strength, abrasivity index, porosity, density, and soundness tests.
Building Material Testing: Concrete, cement, bricks, timber, aggregates, paver blocks, and more.
Chemical Analysis: Testing for construction water, steel, aggregates, fly ash, minerals, ores, and industrial chemicals.
Non-Destructive Testing (NDT): Ultrasonic pulse velocity, rebound hammer, carbonation test, pile integrity testing, and structural assessments.
Concrete & Road Testing: Mix designs, compression tests, pavement design, traffic surveys, and more.
Engineering & Structural Assessments
Condition Assessment: Evaluation of RCC, PCC, and steel structures for buildings, bridges, and underwater structures.
Structural Health Monitoring: Crack width measurement, vibration analysis, deflection measurement, and corrosion analysis.
Geotechnical Investigation: Safe bearing capacity, plate load tests, pile integrity tests, and permeability tests.
Pavement & Runway Testing: Benkelman beam deflection tests, axle load surveys, K-value tests, and deformation modulus analysis.
